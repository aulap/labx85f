FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to get started.

How do I get TraceQuell Free?

Use the Free Download section. When the official direct installer is published, you can download it there; otherwise request a copy via our Contact page. No purchase is required.

Is TraceQuell an antivirus or EDR replacement?

No. TraceQuell helps analysts investigate suspicious Windows activity and correlate evidence alongside existing antivirus, EDR, and SIEM tools.

What do INFO, REVIEW, HIGH, and CRITICAL mean?

They describe investigation priority, not certainty of compromise. Findings explain the recorded activity, while analysts verify context, authorization, and any missing evidence before reaching a conclusion.

Are the detection rules the same in Live and Offline?

TraceQuell applies the same detection logic to comparable event evidence. Results can differ when log coverage, permissions, collection boundaries, or available endpoint context differ. Offline Evidence and Offline Timeline are Business features.

What is the difference between Attack Timeline and Event Correlation?

Attack Timeline shows when activity occurred. Event Correlation focuses on supported multi-event relationships that meet source identity and rule requirements. Events appearing close together do not automatically form a confirmed connection.

Does TraceQuell scan automatically on startup?

No. Event Investigation opens in a ready state. Choose Quick Scan or Full Scan and select Run Scan when you want to collect and analyze live Windows events.

Does TraceQuell work on Windows Server?

TraceQuell includes Windows Server-oriented event investigation for available AD DS, DNS, Hyper-V, and Failover Clustering logs. Actual coverage depends on installed roles, audit policy, permissions, retention, and supported channels. Test it in your environment before operational deployment.

Will it read every historical Windows event?

Not necessarily. Events must still exist and be accessible. Live collection can apply per-source limits to protect performance, and unavailable or truncated evidence should be treated as a coverage limitation.

What is the difference between Individual and Business?

Individual is licensed to one named investigator and includes live investigation, Investigation Case, reporting, exports, and copy tools. Business includes all Individual capabilities plus Offline Evidence and Offline Timeline, and is licensed for authorized internal use within one legal entity.

Which plan should I choose for incident response?

Choose Individual if your work focuses on investigating live Windows endpoints and delivering case reports. Choose Business if your team also needs to analyze collected EVTX files and reconstruct offline event timelines.

Can TraceQuell investigate offline EVTX files?

Yes, with Professional Business. Offline Evidence and Offline Timeline are not included in Free or Professional Individual. Analysis is limited to the EVTX files and event data available.

Does an expired license delete investigation data?

No. An expired Individual or Business license returns the application to Free mode; existing evidence files are not deleted by license expiration. Renewal restores the features included in your plan.
